Scott launches a professional society for Real Estate
=====

### Crowdscreened Groups

This is birth of *crowdscreened groups*, which is a group with the following characteristics:

- membership in the group is well defined.
- Membership can be public facing, e.g. *licensed electricians;* secret society, e.g. *Skull and Bones*; or underground , e.g. *The French Resistance*
- insiders know the criteria very well; outsiders could but probably don’t
- members can recognize and attest to each other peer-to-peer. This is the case even in underground societies like the French Resistance.
- members will be motivated to identify other members, and to identify impostors as such, honestly.

### Creation of Scott’s cGroup

Scott launches the world’s first *crowd-screened group*: a group of pubkeys that is curated by its members. Brainstorm is ideally suited for this. Waaaay more simple than one might think!

Step 1: He cannot find a List Header for Crowdscreened Groups in his concept dictionary so he creates one with `event_id_crowdscreened_groups`.

Step 2: He adds an item to the list of crowd screened groups called Real Estate Developers.

Step 3: He creates a List Header called: *Attestations of Membership to a Crowdscreened Group*. Required tags: p

He then decides that Crowdscreened Groups is so cool that he vibe codes an app called ScottsList where users can peruse all publicly available crowd screened groups. It later turns into a yellow pages where people go to find electricians, plumbers, etc based on their acceptance into the relevant professional society.
